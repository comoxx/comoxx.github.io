Côme Hosxe

Machine learning research engineer. CentraleSupélec and Master MVA.

I am a machine learning engineer with a research background. I studied Mathematics and Data Science at CentraleSupélec and did the Master MVA (Mathematics, Vision, Learning) at ENS Paris-Saclay.

From May to October 2026 I was a research intern at Callyope. I worked on reasoning in audio language models for speech-based mental-health assessment. I fine-tuned models on their own chain-of-thought traces and explored reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (GRPO). I also built an evaluation harness for open audio language models, run at scale on the Jean Zay and Leonardo clusters.

Before that, I worked on bird species classification from audio at the University of Auckland and on retrieval-augmented generation for corporate reports at Datamaran.

I am mostly interested in audio and speech models, reinforcement learning, and how machine learning can serve environmental and social causes.

I am looking for a full-time position from November 2026.
