I am a machine learning engineer with a research background. I studied Mathematics and Data Science at [CentraleSupélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/) and did the [Master MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) (Mathematics, Vision, Learning) at ENS Paris-Saclay.

Most recently I was a research intern at [Callyope](https://callyope.com/), working on reasoning in audio language models for mental-health assessment from speech. Before that, I worked on bird sound classification at the [University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/) and on retrieval-augmented generation at [Datamaran](https://www.datamaran.com/).

I am mostly interested in audio and speech models, reinforcement learning, and how machine learning can serve environmental and social causes.

I am looking for a full-time position from November 2026.
