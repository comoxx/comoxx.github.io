I am a machine learning engineer with a research background. I studied Mathematics and Data Science at [CentraleSupélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/) and did the [Master MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) (Mathematics, Vision, Learning) at ENS Paris-Saclay.

From May to October 2026 I was a research intern at [Callyope](https://callyope.com/). I worked on reasoning in audio language models for speech-based mental-health assessment. I fine-tuned models on their own chain-of-thought traces and explored reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (GRPO). I also built an evaluation harness for open audio language models, run at scale on the Jean Zay and Leonardo clusters.

Before that, I worked on bird species classification from audio at the [University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/) and on retrieval-augmented generation for corporate reports at [Datamaran](https://www.datamaran.com/).

I am mostly interested in audio and speech models, reinforcement learning, and how machine learning can serve environmental and social causes.

I am looking for a full-time position from November 2026.
